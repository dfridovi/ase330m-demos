ASE 330M Demos

Interactive tools for linear systems in aerospace engineering.

Modal decomposition

  • Aircraft Dynamics Explorer
    Adjust physical aircraft parameters and see the resulting longitudinal and lateral-directional modes, 3D animation, and time-series charts.
  • Double Pendulum: Normal Modes
    Adjust masses and rod lengths and watch the linearized double pendulum decompose into a slow in-phase mode and a fast anti-phase mode, with side-by-side animation and time-series charts.
  • Clohessy-Wiltshire Relative Orbital Motion
    Adjust a chaser satellite's initial relative position and velocity and watch its motion relative to a chief in orbit — a drifting or closed 2:1 ellipse, plus a decoupled cross-track oscillation at the same frequency.

Forced response

  • Quarter-Car Forced Response
    A mechanic pushes on the hood to test the shocks: adjust spring stiffness and damping on a forced spring-mass-damper and compare its step, impulse, and frequency response, all tracing back to the same eigenvalues of the state matrix.

Control design

  • Flight Control: Phugoid State Feedback
    Design a linear state-feedback controller u = -K(x - x_ref) to damp the phugoid mode, then compare closed-loop poles/zeros, frequency response, and tracking error against a 3D-animated aircraft response.
  • Satellite Rendezvous: CW State Feedback
    Design a state-feedback controller u = -Kx to bring a trailing chaser satellite to within a few meters of the chief, then compare your gain's fuel cost against a naive and a tuned reference design in 2D, 3D, and time-domain views.
