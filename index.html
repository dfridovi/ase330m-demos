ASE 330M Demos

Interactive tools for linear systems in aerospace engineering.

  • Aircraft Dynamics Explorer
    Adjust physical aircraft parameters and see the resulting longitudinal and lateral-directional modes, 3D animation, and time-series charts.
  • Double Pendulum: Normal Modes
    Adjust masses and rod lengths and watch the linearized double pendulum decompose into a slow in-phase mode and a fast anti-phase mode, with side-by-side animation and time-series charts.
  • Clohessy-Wiltshire Relative Orbital Motion
    Adjust a chaser satellite's initial relative position and velocity and watch its motion relative to a chief in orbit — a drifting or closed 2:1 ellipse, plus a decoupled cross-track oscillation at the same frequency.
